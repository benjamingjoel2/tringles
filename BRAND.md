# Brand brief: Tringles

This is the single source of truth every agent reads before working on this project.
To use the agent team on another brand, copy `.claude/agents/` into that repo and write
a `BRAND.md` with the same headings.

## What it is

Small-group singles trips. Seven nights, sixteen members (8 women + 8 men; 6–8 + 6–8 in
Nairobi), one host, a deck of cards and some of the most romantic places on earth.
Season one: Lisbon, Rio de Janeiro, Bali, Nairobi.

- Tagline: **Skip the first date. Take the trip.**
- Promise: meeting someone is part of the itinerary, never the whole point of it.
- Positioning: a dating show without the cruelty. Nobody gets sent home, picks stay
  private, only mutual matches are revealed.
- Audience: singles roughly 26–38 (28–40 for Nairobi) who would rather meet someone
  over a kayak than a chat window. English- and Russian-speaking seasons.
- Founders: a travel operator (partners in 130+ countries) and a dating creator.

## Voice

- Plain, concrete, warm. Short sentences. Name the real thing: "horseback through the
  estate, then a picnic", not "unforgettable experiences".
- Confident without hype: no exclamation marks, no "amazing", "epic", "once in a
  lifetime", "curated", "elevate", "unlock".
- Calm about safety and consent. State the rule plainly ("You can pass on any card,
  game or date") rather than reassuring at length.
- Playful in the mechanics (cards, challenges, the Reveal), never at a member's expense.
- No em dashes in site copy; use commas, colons or full stops. En dash for ranges
  (26–38, Sat 15 – Sat 22 May).
- Prices in euros with a comma: €3,600. Always "per person, land only".
- Capitalised program names: The Reveal, Date Picks, The Long Table, The Cards, the
  Secret Recipe Challenge, host's circle (lower case).
- Policy text not yet reviewed by a lawyer is labelled "Draft". Never remove that label
  without the owner's say-so.

## Non-negotiables

- Safety promises stay true everywhere they appear: application + host call, ID check,
  signed code of conduct, single room with own key, trained host who can remove anyone,
  no game involves alcohol, licensed transfers, filming only with a signed release and
  never on private dates.
- Never invent facts: partners, testimonials, press, member numbers, prices or dates.
  If copy needs one, leave a clearly marked placeholder like `[TBC: price]` and say so.
- Photos are Wikimedia Commons with credits in `CREDITS` in `app.js`. Any new image needs
  a licence and a credit entry.

## Visual system

- Grand-hotel feel: 12-column grid, 22px outer margins, full-height photography, serif
  display (IBM Plex Serif) over Montserrat body, single light theme by design.
- Core tokens live in `:root` in `styles.css`: stone `#F4F4F2`, ink `#1A1A1A`,
  terra/brick `#9E483F`, sage `#9AA67C`, slate `#8590A3`, tan/blush `#DFC8B7`.
- One tinted band per trip: Lisbon sage, Rio brick, Bali slate, Nairobi blush.
- Pills for actions, small tag-links for "Explore" style links, uppercase eyebrows.
